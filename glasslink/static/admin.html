Displays

Manage displays

A new display gets 768 x 768 and the next free off-screen parking slot. Then press Learn (captain seat) for a captain side display or Learn (FO seat) for an FO side display, and Right-Alt + click it once when the banner says so. The DMC sets the camera itself.

Camera after a pop-out

The key that loads your own flying view (a sim custom camera), exactly as bound in the sim's controls. The DMC presses it when it has finished popping out displays. Empty = stay in the pilot seat view.
