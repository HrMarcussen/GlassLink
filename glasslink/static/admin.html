Displays

Manage displays

A new display gets 768 x 768 and a free parking place off-screen. Press Learn on it and Right-Alt + click that display once in the cockpit when the banner says so: the DMC sets the camera itself and pops it out on its own from then on.

Camera after a pop-out

The key that loads your own flying view (a sim custom camera), exactly as bound in the sim's controls. The DMC presses it when it has finished popping out displays. Empty = stay in the pilot seat view.
